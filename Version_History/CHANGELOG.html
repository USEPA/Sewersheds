Changelog

All releases of the EPA national sewershed dataset. Each version’s data and full release notes are in Version_History/V_<version>/; the current release is also in Current_Release.zip.

[1.3] — October 2026

Focus: complete and correct the sourced sewersheds, and remove all overlaps. Full notes: Version_History/V_1_3/readme.md.

Added

  • 56 plants that were modeled in v1.2 now have a sourced boundary; 270 sewersheds in total have a boundary added or replaced in v1.3 (Version_Added = 1.3).
  • New sources: 34 plants from 23 utility, county and regional datasets found online (CO, FL, IN, ME, MS, MT, OH, TX, VA), including sewer lines buffered by 150 m; boundaries digitized or split by EPA for Lorain County OH, Opelika AL, Cary NC, North Shore Sanitary District IL, San Antonio TX and Woodsville NH.
  • sources.csv documents the source of every sourced sewershed (3,293 plants; previously only recent additions).
  • New sewershed fields: Flag, Source_Dataset, Source, Geometry_From.
  • Census crosswalks: Population_Multiplier, Building_Multiplier and Weight_Method.

Changed

  • Massachusetts sewersheds replaced with MassDEP Estimated Sewer System Service Area Boundaries (MassGIS PubV2025_10): collection areas joined to the plant they drain to.
  • New Hampshire sewersheds replaced with NH DES sewer service areas (sewer and combined water/sewer areas merged; shared areas split by town); Woodsville digitized from local maps.
  • Sonoma County, CA: 10 sewersheds corrected for a ~130 m positional offset in v1.2.
  • San Antonio, TX: SAWS territory split between Steven M. Clouse (formerly Dos Rios) and Leon Creek (approximate); Martinez I/II/IV and Salitrillo from a new source; ten surrounding cities refreshed.
  • Overlaps removed: sourced boundaries take priority over modeled ones; overlapping sourced boundaries are merged, nested, trimmed of slivers or split halfway between plants; overlapping modeled sewersheds are split halfway between plants. About 7,270 km² of overlap in v1.2; none in v1.3.
  • Census crosswalks: Crosswalk_Multiplier replaced by two multipliers; blocks with population but no building footprints use area weighting instead of being dropped; sewersheds crossing a state line are counted in every state; buildings in non-residential blocks are counted.
  • Pop_2020 and Buildings recomputed for every sewershed from the v1.3 crosswalk.

Removed

  • 31 boundaries are no longer treated as sourced (29 in South Carolina, North Port FL, Snyderville Basin UT). Their v1.2 polygon is kept with Method = Modeled and Flag = needs_v2_model.
  • 68 small modeled sewersheds that lay entirely inside a sourced sewershed, and Hopkinton, MA (no MassDEP service area), no longer have a polygon.

[1.2] — May 21, 2026

  • 3,253 sourced and 13,826 modeled sewersheds (19% sourced).
